Dotsev AV, Moradi MH, Deniskova TE, Esmailizadeh A, Bakoev NF, Koshkina OA, Griffin DK, Romanov MN, Zinovieva NA.
Molecular Genetic Assessment Aids in Clarifying Phylogenetic Status of Iranian Kerman Wild Sheep.
Animals 15(2): 238 (2025). https://doi.org/10.3390/ani15020238

kerman-wild-sheep-fst.nex: the pairwise F_ST values among the 8 populations (Kerman wild sheep, the Asiatic
mouflon O. gmelini and six urial groups; SNP genotypes), Supplementary Table S1 (animals-15-00238-s001.zip),
the input of the Neighbor-Net of the paper's Figure 4 (SplitsTree 4.14.6). The SNP data are on Figshare,
https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.28124693.
