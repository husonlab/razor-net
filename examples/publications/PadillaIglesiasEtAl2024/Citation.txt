Data in this directory is from the following paper (please cite it as source):

Padilla-Iglesias, C., Blanco-Portillo, J., Pricop, B. et al. Deep history of cultural and linguistic evolution among Central African hunter-gatherers.
Nat Hum Behav 8, 1263–1275 (2024). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-01891-y
Data and code: https://github.com/ceciliapad/GenCultEvo
(the repository states no licence; the matrices here are our own computations from its tables, as described
below)

Ten Central African hunter-gatherer groups (Aka, Babongo, Baka, Bakola, Bakoya, Batwa (East), Batwa (West),
Bedzan, Efe, Sua). The paper's Figure 2 shows three NeighborNet graphs: musical instruments, genes and
subsistence tools, built from dimensionality-reduced (PCoA) distance matrices.

hunter-gatherers-music-jaccard.nex, hunter-gatherers-tools-jaccard.nex: Jaccard distances (1 - shared / union)
between the presence-absence repertoires of 44 musical instruments and of 40 subsistence tools (files
data/P_A_music.csv, data/P_A_subsistence.csv of the repository; two missing entries of the music table ignored
pairwise), the distances the paper reduced before drawing; computed here directly, without the reduction.
hunter-gatherers-genetics-fst.nex: pairwise Hudson F_ST between the groups on their hunter-gatherer ancestry
segments (data/fst_masked.csv, with the Mbendjele column dropped as in the authors' code, since it duplicates
the Aka; no genetic data for the Western Batwa; Efe and Sua coincide, they form the Mbuti).
